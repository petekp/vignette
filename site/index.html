Works the way you’re used to

Vignette is first and foremost a seamless replacement for the screenshot tool built into macOS. It keeps what’s best about it and refines the rest.

On your clipboard at once

macOS’s thumbnail holds the file for about 5 seconds. Vignette copies each screenshot as soon as it’s saved, ready to paste.

Your recent screenshots

Double-tap right Shift and the last ones you took slide in as cards at the edge of your screen, newest at the bottom. You can copy, draw on or delete any of them, or select several to copy or delete them together.

Draw on any of them

Click a card to draw a box, an arrow or a note on it. Press Return to copy it with your drawing.

Stitch several into one

Select a few cards and press Stitch. It joins them into one image and numbers each piece. Claude shrinks large images before it reads them, so Stitch picks the layout that keeps the most detail.

Drag a card out

Drag a card into Claude Code, a chat or Finder. If you drew on the card, the image you drop has your drawing on it.

Screen recordings too

Recordings from Cmd+Shift+5 come in as cards, with their length on them. Click one to open it.

Draw it for your agent

Send gives your drawing to the Claude Code or Codex session you were just in, with a message if you type one. Your agent can answer with a screenshot it drew on. Its marks have their own colour, so you can tell them from yours. Reply sends your answer back to that session.

Send and Reply are experimental. They work through a plugin for Claude Code and Codex, which Vignette offers to install when you first open it.

Made to feel like part of your Mac

Your app stays in front
The stack opens without taking focus from the app you’re in. Close it and you’re right where you were.
Motion you can interrupt
Cards fly into the editor and back on a spring. Interrupt one and it turns around in mid-air.
Marks that stand out
A thin white edge keeps every mark visible on any screenshot, dark or light.
Change it later
Vignette keeps your drawing separate from the screenshot, so you can open it again and move, change or delete any mark.
Several in a row
Select several cards and press Return. Copy or send one, and the next one opens.
Your agent can use it too
Every action is a vignette:// URL, so your agent can show you screenshots and ask you to draw on them.
