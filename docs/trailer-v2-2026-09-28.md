# Trailer, second version (2026-09-28)

Pete's review of the 38 s cut found four problems. This plan fixes each one. The story change needs
his choice before a take.

## What is wrong

1. **The camera leaves too soon.** Moves start within half a second of the thing they show, and
   settle over 1.2 to 1.8 s, so the viewer never sees a moment at rest.
2. **The first thumbnail is missed.** At the capture's release the camera frames the game and the
   card's corner tightly, and the driver clicks the thumbnail 0.35 s after it lands.
3. **Send is missed.** The camera leaves the editor 0.1 s after Cmd+Return, for a tight frame on
   the terminal. The card flying home and the drawing arriving in Claude Code are never seen
   together.
4. **Claude's sketch does not follow from your request.** You asked for a moon, a water tower and
   a city. Claude answered with ledges, because the game's hidden rule says the cat must reach the
   moon. The viewer does not know the rule, so the sketch reads as unrelated. Its card also arrives
   in a tight frame, leaves, and comes back through the history, which is three things to follow
   at once.

## The story (Pete chose it, 2026-09-28)

**Claude finds a problem in your request and shows you.** The moon and the ledges are gone from the
story.

| Beat | Caption | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Capture | Take screenshots as usual ⌘⇧4 | The drag, then the thumbnail landing, held 1.3 s before the click. |
| Mark | Mark it up | A big moon, a water tower boxed where the cat lands after its jump, and a city behind. |
| Send | Send it to your agent ⌘↩ | "Add these". Wide shot: the card goes home while the drawing lands in the terminal. |
| Build | It builds what you marked | The game reloads with all three. |
| Sketch | It shows you what it found | Claude's card arrives, wide. A click opens it: an arrow from the cat to the tower, "the tower blocks the jump", and a box, "move it here?". |
| Adjust | Change its sketch | You drag the box to the right roof. |
| Reply | Send it back ↩ | Wide shot: the card goes home, the reply lands, and the tower moves to the right roof. |
| History | Double-tap right Shift for your recent screenshots ⇧⇧ | The stack, with every card from the take. |

Why it follows: the problem is in the thing you asked for. The viewer sees the cat, the arrow and
the tower, so no rule needs explaining. Claude asks instead of moving the tower because where things
stand is your decision, which the game's `CLAUDE.md` says.

## The camera

- **Settle, then hold.** Moves settle over 2 s instead of 1.2 to 1.8 s. Nothing moves until the
  thing it shows has been still on screen for at least 1 s.
- **Capture:** wide at the release, on the game and the whole corner, and the driver waits 1.2 s
  before clicking the thumbnail.
- **Send and Reply:** the whole desktop from the key press, so the editor closing, the card flying
  home, and the line arriving in the terminal are in one frame. Then a slow move in on the terminal.
- **Claude's card:** the game and the corner, from before the card arrives until it is opened.

The trailer grows from 38 s to about 50 s.

## Changes

- `beats.toml`: the message, the captions, the spans, and every camera move.
- `drive.py`: the thumbnail waits before the click, Claude's card is opened by a click, and a last
  beat opens the history.
- `stage/mew/CLAUDE.md`: the cat's first jump and where it lands, the check after every change, and
  what the sketch holds. The moon rule and the ledges are gone.
- New takes, cut and reviewed frame by frame, then `site/` and the README poster again.

## Results (2026-09-28)

Two takes. Both passed every beat, and Claude found the blocked jump and sketched it as asked: an
arrow from the cat to the tower, "the tower blocks the jump", and a box on the middle roof, "move it
here?". The second take is the cut: 47.7 s, 1600 by 1000 at 60 fps.

- **The first take's drag missed.** It pressed inside Claude's box, which draws a new box, so Claude
  moved the tower to its own suggestion. The driver takes the box by its outline again.
- **Spans 4 and 5 overlapped** when Claude was idle as soon as the drag ended. Span 5 now starts
  when the take sees Claude idle, which is always after the drag's pause.
- **The poster** is the three marks with the tower's box beside the cat, under "Mark it up".
