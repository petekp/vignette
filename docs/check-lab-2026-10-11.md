# One success check, lit as metal, and a Check Lab to tune it (2026-10-11)

## What changes

- **One check.** `MetalCheck` draws both: the badge on a send's logo (15 pt) and the Copied notice
  (22 pt, which Stitch's "Copied" uses too). Every length in it is a fraction of its diameter, so
  the two sizes look the same. The failure marks (Not copied, and a send's clock and warnings) stay
  SF Symbols: they are not checks.
- **Lit, not painted.** A shader (`CheckMetal.metal`) shapes the disc as a shallow dome with a
  rounded rim and raises the check mark out of it with a bevelled edge, from the SF Symbol's own
  shape. Every point is lit by what it reflects: a studio with a bright ceiling, a soft horizon well
  below eye level, a dim floor, and a key light, a soft box shaped like a window whose reflection
  lands on the upper face. The metal reflects the studio in its own colour face on and white at a
  grazing angle (Schlick's Fresnel), so the rim brightens toward its edge and the dome carries the
  ceiling, the horizon and the floor across its face. A clear coat over the metal reflects the
  lights white, as glossy paint does. Roughness softens every reflection. The raised mark shades
  the disc on its far side from the light.
- **The glare is a light, not a stripe.** A strip light sweeps across the studio once as the check
  appears. Its reflection bends over the dome and flashes along the rim and the mark's bevel where
  they turn toward it. The sweep's place comes from a clock read every frame (`TimelineView`),
  since a shader argument does not interpolate under `withAnimation`. With no motion there is no
  glare, as before.
- **The bloom follows the light.** A bright pass (`checkBright`) keeps what is brighter than a
  threshold, SwiftUI's Gaussian blur spreads it, and it is added over the check, so the lights'
  reflections, the glare's most of all, glow past the disc's edge. The check is drawn in a square
  wider than the disc by the bloom's reach on each side. A blur sampled in the shader was tried
  first: its taps, a few points apart, caught thin highlights such as the mark's edge and drew ghost
  copies of them across the disc.
- **Its look is settings.** The `check` keys in `ui`, so a value tuned in the lab is what every card
  draws, and tuned values are promoted to the defaults like any other tweak.
- **The Check Lab** (`vignette://check-lab`, needs `debug`, and Check in the tweak panel) shows the
  check large, and both notices at their real size on a card: the Copied notice, and a send going
  from Sending to Sent. Replay runs them as a card does. Loop runs the glare over and over at a
  chosen speed, and Hold stops it at one place, so the reflection and its bloom can be judged frame
  by frame.

## Settings

| Key | Default | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `checkColor` | `#29b84d` | the disc's metal: the colour it reflects face on |
| `checkMarkColor` | `#eef1f3` | the mark's metal, near white so the mark reads against the green |
| `checkRoughness` | 0.3 | 0 a mirror, 1 brushed |
| `checkDome` | 0.25 | how far the disc's middle rises, a fraction of its radius |
| `checkRim` | 0.18 | the rounded rim's width, a fraction of the radius |
| `checkLightAngle` | -40 | where the key light is, degrees clockwise from the top |
| `checkLightHeight` | 50 | its height above the disc's plane, in degrees |
| `checkLight` | 1.6 | its strength |
| `checkStudio` | 0.75 | how much brighter the studio's ceiling is than its floor |
| `checkMarkSize` | 0.5 | the mark's size, a fraction of the diameter |
| `checkMarkHeight` | 0.06 | how far it stands out of the disc, a fraction of the diameter |
| `checkMarkBevel` | 0.05 | the width of its rounded edge, a fraction of the diameter |
| `checkMarkShadow` | 0.25 | how dark its shadow on the disc is |
| `checkGlare` | 1.4 | the strip light's strength; 0 turns the glare off |
| `checkGlareWidth` | 0.08 | the strip's width as the disc reflects it |
| `checkGlareAngle` | 25 | the strip's tilt from upright, in degrees |
| `checkGlareDuration` | 0.55 s | how long it takes to cross |
| `checkGlareDelay` | 0.12 s | how long after the check appears it starts |
| `checkBloom` | 1 | the bloom's strength; 0 turns it off |
| `checkBloomRadius` | 0.35 | how far the bloom spreads, a fraction of the diameter |
| `checkBloomThreshold` | 0.7 | how bright a reflection must be to bloom |

The duration and the delay are motion and scale with it. The mark's metal is its own colour because
a mark in the disc's green would be lit the same as the disc and read only by its bevel.
