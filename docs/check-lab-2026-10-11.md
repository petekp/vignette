# One success check, and a Check Lab to tune it (2026-10-11)

Pete asked for the check on a sent card and the check on a copied card to be one thing, for a lab to
tune its metal and its glare, and for a bloom on the glare.

## What changes

- **One check.** `MetalCheck` draws both: the badge on a send's logo (15 pt) and the Copied notice
  (22 pt, which Stitch's "Copied" uses too). Every length in it is a fraction of its diameter, so
  the two sizes look the same. The failure marks (Not copied, and a send's clock and warnings) stay
  SF Symbols: they are not checks.
- **Its look is settings.** The metal, the glare and the bloom are `ui` keys with a `check` prefix,
  so a value tuned in the lab is what every card draws, and Pete's tuned values are promoted to the
  defaults like any other tweak. The defaults reproduce the check as it shipped on live-ink
  (3c8ed65), with the bloom added.
- **The glare is a shader.** `CheckGlare.metal` draws the band of light and its bloom in one pass:
  the band's bright part, above a threshold, blurred with a Gaussian and added back. The bloom
  spills past the disc's edge, so the glare is drawn in a frame wider than the check by the bloom's
  radius, outside the disc's clip. The band's place comes from a clock read every frame
  (`TimelineView`), since a shader argument does not interpolate under `withAnimation`. With no
  motion there is no glare and no bloom, as before.
- **The Check Lab** (`vignette://check-lab`, needs `debug`, and a button in the tweak panel) shows
  the check large, and both notices at their real size on a card: the Copied notice, and a send going
  from Sending to Sent. Replay runs them as a card does. Loop runs the glare over and over at a
  chosen speed, and Hold stops it at one place, so the band and its bloom can be judged frame by
  frame.

## Settings

| Key | Default | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `checkColor` | `#29b84d` | the metal's own colour, the middle of its gradient |
| `checkShine` | 0.55 | how far the lit side goes toward white |
| `checkDepth` | 0.45 | how far the far side goes toward black |
| `checkLightAngle` | -45 | where the light comes from, in degrees clockwise from the top |
| `checkRim` | 1 | the bevel's strength: light on the lit edge, shade on the far one |
| `checkRimWidth` | 0.05 | the bevel's width, a fraction of the diameter |
| `checkHighlight` | 0.8 | the fixed highlight's brightness |
| `checkHighlightSize` | 0.53 | its width, a fraction of the diameter |
| `checkGlyphSize` | 0.5 | the check mark's size, a fraction of the diameter |
| `checkGlyphShadow` | 0.35 | the shadow under the check mark |
| `checkGlare` | 0.75 | the band's peak brightness; 0 turns the glare off |
| `checkGlareWidth` | 0.33 | the band's width, a fraction of the diameter |
| `checkGlareAngle` | 25 | the band's tilt from upright, in degrees |
| `checkGlareDuration` | 0.55 s | how long it takes to cross |
| `checkGlareDelay` | 0.12 s | how long after the check appears it starts |
| `checkBloom` | 1 | the bloom's strength; 0 turns it off |
| `checkBloomRadius` | 0.4 | how far the bloom spreads, a fraction of the diameter |
| `checkBloomThreshold` | 0.2 | how bright the band must be to bloom |

The duration and the delay are motion and scale with it. The bloom is on by default because Pete
asked for it before tuning. At its default it adds about a fifth of white at the band's centre and
fades out about 5 pt past the disc's edge on a 22 pt check.
