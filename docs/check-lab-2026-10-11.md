# One success check, a spinning metal coin, and a Check Lab to tune it (2026-10-11)

## What changes

- **One check.** `MetalCheck` draws both: the badge on a send's logo (15 pt) and the Copied notice
  (22 pt, which Stitch's "Copied" uses too). Every length in it is a fraction of its diameter, so
  the two sizes look the same. The failure marks (Not copied, and a send's clock and warnings) stay
  SF Symbols: they are not checks.
- **A solid coin.** A shader (`CheckMetal.metal`) ray marches a green coin with a rounded rim and
  slightly domed faces, and a chrome check mark standing on each face. The back's mark is mirrored,
  so it reads the right way round while the back is toward the viewer. The mark's stroke is a round
  bead, so its top carries reflections. A camera eight radii away looks through the face's plane, so
  the front face at rest fills the layout's disc exactly, and a turned coin foreshortens as a real
  one does. Each pixel averages four rays, since the mark's outline crosses the face inside the
  coin.
- **Lit by a studio.** Every point is lit by what it reflects: a bright ceiling, a soft horizon
  below eye level, a dim floor, a key light shaped like a window, and a tall strip light to one side.
  The metal reflects the studio in its own colour face on and white at a grazing angle (Schlick's
  Fresnel). A clear coat on the green reflects the lights white, as glossy paint does. Roughness
  softens every reflection. The mark casts a soft shadow on the face, and the face darkens where it
  meets the mark.
- **It spins as it appears.** The coin turns about its vertical axis and settles face on with a
  spring that rocks it slightly past. Its face and rim flash as they pass the strip light. The turn
  comes from a clock read every frame (`TimelineView`), since a shader argument does not
  interpolate under `withAnimation`. Once settled, the clock stops. With no motion, the coin does
  not spin.
- **The bloom follows the light.** A bright pass (`checkBright`) keeps what is brighter than a
  threshold. SwiftUI's Gaussian blur spreads it, and it is added over the coin, so the reflections
  of the lights glow past its edge. The coin is drawn in a square wider than the disc by the bloom's
  reach on each side. A blur sampled in the shader drew ghost copies of thin highlights, which is
  why the blur is SwiftUI's.
- **Its look is settings.** The `check` keys live in `ui`, so a value tuned in the lab is what every
  card draws. Tuned values are promoted to the defaults like any other tweak.
- **The Check Lab** (`vignette://check-lab`, needs `debug`, and Check in the tweak panel) shows the
  coin at 120 pt, and both notices at their real size on a card: the Copied notice, and a send going
  from Sending to Sent. Once runs the spin as a card does. Loop runs it over and over at a chosen
  speed. Hold stops it at one place, so a reflection and its bloom can be judged frame by frame.

## Why a shader rather than SceneKit or RealityKit

The coin is a 15 pt badge inside a SwiftUI card, and a card can show several at once. A shader is
one SwiftUI modifier, drawn in the card's own layer and composited with the bloom like any other
view. A SceneKit or RealityKit view is an AppKit view with its own Metal layer and render loop, one
per badge, and its lighting would still need the studio's reflections built as an environment map.

## Settings

| Key | Default | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `checkColor` | `#29b84d` | the coin's metal: the colour it reflects face on |
| `checkMarkColor` | `#eef1f3` | the mark's metal, near white so the mark reads against the green |
| `checkRoughness` | 0.3 | 0 a mirror, 1 brushed |
| `checkThickness` | 0.14 | the coin's thickness, a fraction of the diameter |
| `checkEdge` | 0.05 | the rounding of its rim, a fraction of the diameter |
| `checkDome` | 0.06 | how far each face rises at its centre, a fraction of the diameter |
| `checkMarkSize` | 0.52 | the mark's width, a fraction of the diameter |
| `checkMarkWeight` | 0.1 | its stroke's width, a fraction of the diameter |
| `checkMarkHeight` | 0.05 | how far it stands off the face, a fraction of the diameter |
| `checkMarkShadow` | 0.7 | how dark its shadow on the face is |
| `checkLightAngle` | -40 | where the key light is, degrees clockwise from the top |
| `checkLightHeight` | 68 | its height above the face, in degrees |
| `checkLight` | 1.6 | its strength |
| `checkGlint` | 1.5 | the strip light's strength; the spin sweeps the coin past it |
| `checkGlintAngle` | 60 | where the strip stands, degrees to the right of the viewer |
| `checkStudio` | 0.75 | how much brighter the studio's ceiling is than its floor |
| `checkSpinTurns` | 1 | how many times the coin turns; 0 for no spin |
| `checkSpinDuration` | 1.1 s | the spring's duration |
| `checkSpinBounce` | 0.2 | how far it rocks past face on before it settles |
| `checkSpinDelay` | 0.05 s | how long after the check appears the spin starts |
| `checkBloom` | 1 | the bloom's strength; 0 turns it off |
| `checkBloomRadius` | 0.35 | how far the bloom spreads, a fraction of the diameter |
| `checkBloomThreshold` | 0.7 | how bright a reflection must be to bloom |

The spin's duration and delay are motion, and they scale with it. The default spring settles about
1.9 s after the check appears. The mark has its own metal because a mark in the coin's green would be
lit the same as the face and read only by its edges.
