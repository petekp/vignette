# A mark's colour says who drew it

A person's marks are red and every agent's are indigo. A white edge around every mark gives the
contrast against any background. The colour is not stored, and a colour an agent asks for is
ignored. Picking each mark's hue for contrast had made one drawing red, yellow and light blue, and a
reader could not tell that the difference meant nothing.

## Considered options

- A palette the person picks from.
- A hue picked for each mark from the background behind it.
- Violet for agents, and blue, which is too close to the selection outline.
